# Deadweight Removal — product

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), no framework, no tracking, system fonts
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (a one-page pitch site)

## Users
1. **The owner of Deadweight Removal** (name unknown), who first sees this as a free sample linked from Facebook, on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser. The page has to make the business look established within one screen.
2. **Their customers:** homeowners and businesses in South Jersey with furniture, appliances, a basement, a shed or a yard to clear. They arrive from Facebook posts on their phones and want to know "do they take my stuff, and how do I reach them" in seconds.

## Purpose
Turn a business with zero web presence (no site, no Google Maps listing, no reviews; source: Muse brief 2026-10-08) into one that looks established next to competitors with real sites (Junk Lion, CamoVets), and get customers to call or text.

## Positioning
Local, fast, flexible junk removal. Flyer wording: "SAME DAY SERVICE", "FLEXIBLE SCHEDULING", "RELIABLE & PROFESSIONAL", "INSURED", "5% DISCOUNT CASH", "SERVING SOUTH JERSEY & SURROUNDING AREAS", "CALL OR TEXT 609-852-8552".

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: phone 609-852-8552, calls and texts ("CALL OR TEXT", their flyer). No email, no booking link.
- Services (flyer, verbatim): furniture removal, appliance removal, basement cleanouts, yard debris removal, shed cleanouts, misc. junk removal; commercial and residential.
- Area: "South Jersey & surrounding areas". Found in a Marlton NJ (Burlington County) group; exact towns unknown.
- Unknown: hours, owner name, years in business, prices (except 5% cash discount), email, reviews.
- No work photos available.

## Brand commitments
- Logo (Facebook profile image, 2026-10-08): a chrome/steel strongman breaking chains between rubble blocks, "DEADWEIGHT" in distressed steel lettering, "REMOVAL" in electric blue, on a blue diamond badge over a dark navy vignette.
- Palette inferred from the logo: deep navy, electric blue, brushed steel silver. (Inferred.)
- Voice: all-caps, punchy flyer copy. (Their flyer.)

## Evidence on hand
- Logo image (720×720, from Facebook og:image).
- Brief facts above. The promo flyer image itself could not be fetched (needs a logged-in browser), so "FREE ESTIMATES" (OCR garble) is not confirmed and not used.

## Principles
- Phone first: offer, area, and call/text on the first phone screen.
- Only real facts; no invented prices, reviews, years, or towns.
- Call and text are the primary actions; text is pre-filled and asks for a photo, since junk jobs are naturally quoted from photos (Muse's suggestion, inferred fit).
